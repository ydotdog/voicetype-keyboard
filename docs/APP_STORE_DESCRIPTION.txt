Turn speech into text in the apps you use every day.

VoiceType is a pay-as-you-go dictation keyboard for iPhone and iPad. Open VoiceType, turn on keyboard mic, then return to an editable text field in another app. Start and finish a clip from the VoiceType keyboard, and keep the field open while your words are transcribed and inserted.

YOUR WORDS, YOUR WORKFLOW
Choose your languages and add names or phrases to your personal vocabulary. Review transcripts in History, make corrections, or copy text when you need it. Failed recordings are kept privately on your device so you can retry or delete them.

YOU CONTROL THE MICROPHONE
Choose a 5-minute, 12-hour or untimed microphone session. Turn it off in VoiceType or from the Live Activity. The microphone stays active between clips while the session is on; only clips you start and finish are uploaded. System interruptions can end a session.

CLEAR PRIVACY CHOICES
Cloud dictation sends your selected audio, language choices and vocabulary hints to VoiceType and OpenAI after your permission. Withdraw permission in Settings. Basic typing works without cloud transcription, Full Access or an internet connection. VoiceType does not collect text you type in other apps.

PAY AS YOU GO
Buy consumable credit packs in the App Store and use credit for transcription. No subscription or automatic top-up. Cloud dictation requires an internet connection, microphone permission, keyboard Full Access and sufficient credit.

Third-party keyboards are unavailable in secure fields and in apps that do not support them.
