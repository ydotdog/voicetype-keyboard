# VoiceType Support

Public URL: https://apeonwheels.com/voicetype/support/

VoiceType is a speech-to-text keyboard for iPhone and iPad, provided by Apeonwheels Inc.

## Contact

For help with VoiceType, billing, account deletion, or privacy, email [kq@apeonwheels.com](mailto:kq@apeonwheels.com). English and Chinese are welcome. Include your device model, iOS version, VoiceType version, and a short description of the problem. Do not send your Apple password, payment card details, or sensitive recordings.

## Set up the keyboard

1. Open VoiceType and sign in with Apple.
2. In iOS Settings, open **General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard**, and add VoiceType.
3. Open the VoiceType keyboard's settings and enable **Allow Full Access** so the keyboard can exchange recording commands and transcripts with the app.
4. In VoiceType, turn on the keyboard microphone and allow microphone access when iOS asks.
5. Return to the app where you want to type. Use the globe key to select VoiceType, tap its microphone to begin a clip, and tap again to finish and insert the transcription.

When the microphone session is off, tapping the microphone in the VoiceType keyboard opens VoiceType and starts the session. Return to your writing app manually. The session uses your saved session length.

## Microphone sessions

The microphone stays active while you use other apps so you can dictate multiple clips during the selected session. Only clips you actively start are submitted for transcription. A clip can finish when you tap to finish, turn off the microphone, reach a clip or session time limit, or encounter an audio interruption. Set **Session length** in VoiceType; starting or stopping a clip does not restart that timer. To end the session, open VoiceType and tap **Turn off keyboard mic**. iOS may end a session after an interruption, including a phone call. Return to VoiceType to start it again.

If microphone access is denied, enable it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → VoiceType. If another app or a call is using audio, finish that activity and try again. Third-party keyboards may be unavailable in secure fields or apps that disable them; use the system keyboard in those fields.

## Transcription and saved recordings

Transcription requires an internet connection and sufficient credit. Audio is processed by our server and OpenAI. Check the connection and available credit if a transcription does not finish. Unfinished or failed recordings appear in History for retry or deletion. Retrying requires you to be signed in to the same VoiceType account. A successfully transcribed recording is removed from the retry queue. Signing out explicitly removes saved retry audio; an expired sign-in preserves it for recovery after you sign in again.

## Languages and personal vocabulary

Choose transcription languages and manage your personal vocabulary in Settings. Words you save help the service recognize names and phrases. You can review suggestions from your own History corrections, remove words, or turn off learning. VoiceType does not learn from text you type in other apps.

## Purchases and refunds

VoiceType uses consumable credit packs purchased through Apple's App Store. The confirmation sheet shows the applicable price before you buy. There is no automatic credit refill or recurring subscription. If a completed purchase has not appeared, relaunch VoiceType while signed in and connected to the internet so pending transactions can be processed. Contact us if the balance is still incorrect.

Apple handles refund requests at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/). A refunded pack's credits are removed. If some were already used, your balance can become negative and sufficient credit is required before another transcription. A reversed refund restores the removed credits. We never charge your payment method automatically.

## Delete your account

Open VoiceType → **Settings → Delete account** and confirm. This removes the account, remaining credit, and transcription history from the active service. The action cannot be undone and does not automatically refund purchases. The [Privacy Policy](https://apeonwheels.com/voicetype/privacy/) explains retained welcome-credit abuse-prevention markers and backups. Contact us if you cannot access the app to make a deletion request.
